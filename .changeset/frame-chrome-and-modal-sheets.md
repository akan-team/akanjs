Frame chrome renders on the server: `Layout.Navbar`, `TopInset`, `TopLeftAction`, `BottomInset` and `BottomTab` are in the SSR HTML in their slot's box and move into the slot on mount, and the top-left action no longer scrolls away. `Layout.Navbar` draws its `title` / `left` / `right` slots and takes `backFallback`; `Link.Back` takes `fallback` (`router.backOrFallback`), and `router.canGoBack()` follows the history entry an SSR navigation writes. `Modal`, `Dialog.Modal`, `Model.New` and the model modals take `presentation` (`"dialog" | "sheet" | "auto"`, a full-screen sheet below `md` that a drag down dismisses) and `placement` (`"center" | "right"`). `topInset` / `bottomInset` take `{ base, sm?, md?, lg?, xl?, "2xl"? }`; a CSR page whose route declares none takes the height of its chrome; on the web a declared `safeArea` pads with `env()`; the frame publishes `--akan-content-height` and `--akan-keyboard-inset`, and mirrors the current page's frame onto `<html>`. `useVisualViewportFrame` (akanjs/webkit) reports the web keyboard, which now reaches `st.keyboardHeight` and keyboard-sticky chrome on SSR pages. The agent launcher clears the bottom chrome and the safe area, sits under menus and modals, and takes `launcherPlacement` / `hideLauncherBelow`. `Link`'s active match strips the basePath and takes `activeMatch`. Upgrade note: `Link`'s prefix match is by path segment and `/` matches only itself; `Portal`'s server capture (`ServerPortal`) is gone.
