Akan.js now requires Bun 1.4.4: every package's `engines.bun` reads `>=1.4.4`. akan-native's doctor fails on an older Bun and warns on any other, with `bun upgrade --version 1.4.4` as the fix. Before 1.4.4, bun:sqlite counted the rows a full-text index's triggers wrote, so `updateManyByQuery` and `removeManyByQuery` on a model with search fields reported far more `matchedCount` and `modifiedCount` than the documents they changed.

Upgrade note: 3.0.2 accepted Bun 1.4.0; run `bun upgrade --version 1.4.4` first.
