The `akan-ui` skill opens by saying who needs it: a page, or a `ui/` component bound to no model, is covered by the `ssr`, `style` and `tsx` rules loaded with the file, and the skill is for a module's own components, lists and forms. Its description says the same, the role table moves under "A module's components", and the lines the `ssr` and `style` rules already hold are gone. The `akan-page` skill's shallow-routing line now says that `usePageLocation().searchParams` follows the replace and the back and forward buttons. Run `akan agent install` in a workspace to pick up the new files.
