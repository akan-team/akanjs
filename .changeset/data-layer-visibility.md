An operator can name the slowest SQL statement and the endpoint that sent it: every statement is counted by its shape, a slow one writes one log line, a local run warns of a full table scan, and the reconnect replay and the Redis publish buffer get gauges.

- **One timing point per driver.** `BunSqliteStatement` and the Postgres statement time each statement where it is sent, and the `db:<model>.<op>` spans, the new counters and the slow-statement log all read that one point. A statement's write-turn wait (the SQLite writer turn, the Postgres pool) is timed apart from its run; a transaction's wait shows on its `BEGIN`.
- **Statements are named by fingerprint**: 12 hex characters of a SHA-1 of the text with every literal `?` and each placeholder, `IN (…)` or repeated `VALUES` list collapsed to one, so `LIMIT 10` and `LIMIT 20` are one statement. The text sent is unchanged. `StatementFingerprint` keeps at most 500 shapes and 2,000 texts.
- **New series**, labelled `fingerprint`, `model` and `op` only: `akan_db_statements_total`, `akan_db_statement_seconds_total`, `akan_db_statement_wait_seconds_total`, `akan_db_statement_rows_total` and the `akan_db_statement_duration_seconds` histogram (16 buckets, 0.1 ms to 10 s). A statement sent outside a model call is named by its table and verb (`memo.select`).
- **`GET /_akan/app/statements`**, behind the metrics token, answers the 20 statements with the most total time and the 20 slowest at p95, each with its normalized text, model, op, wait, rows and its top 3 senders. A process keeps 500 statements, the cheapest going first; behind a gateway each replica sends its top 50 with every report and the gateway adds them by fingerprint.
- **`AKAN_LOG_SLOW_STATEMENT_MS`** (default 200; `off` or `0` turns it off) writes one `SlowStatement` warning for a statement whose wait and run reach it, at most once a minute per fingerprint, with `suppressed` for the lines held back, `sentBy` (`none` outside a request) and the request's `traceId`.
- **A query-plan check in the local dev runtime** explains a statement the first time its fingerprint runs and again after a schema change (`EXPLAIN QUERY PLAN` on SQLite, `EXPLAIN` on Postgres) and warns once, as `QueryPlan`, on a full table scan or a temporary sort. It skips tables under 1,000 rows, inserts, an unfiltered `count`, migrations, tables no model owns, the search tables and in-memory SQLite. `AKAN_QUERY_PLAN_IGNORE=<fingerprint>,…` accepts a deliberate scan; `all` turns the check off.
- **`akan_room_replay_bytes` and `akan_pubsub_buffered_messages`** gauge the frame bytes kept for reconnecting sockets and the publishes waiting for the Redis link; the metrics report carries them as `replayBytes` and `pubsubBufferedCount`. `WebSocketRedisAdaptor.bufferedCount` and the optional `WebsocketAdaptor.bufferedCount` expose the buffer depth.
- No value, id or user data reaches a series, the report or either log line; a Postgres plan is scrubbed of literals before it is logged.

**Upgrading.** Under `AKAN_TRACE=1` or `x-akan-debug`, a `db:<model>.<op>` span, the request line's `db` and `dbMs` and the `Server-Timing` db entries now count SQL statements, not model calls: a call that sends two statements shows two spans (a store that sends none through the SQLite or Postgres driver still shows one per call), and `dbMs` includes the time spent waiting for the write turn or a pool connection. A dashboard reading `db` as a count of model calls reads higher. A statement taking 200 ms or longer now writes a `SlowStatement` warning; set `AKAN_LOG_SLOW_STATEMENT_MS` higher or to `off` to keep the old log. A custom `WebsocketAdaptor` needs no change; `bufferedCount` is optional.
