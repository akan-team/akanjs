The agent guidelines say four more things. A guard that must tell its caller why it refused throws a module-keyed
`Err.Forbidden` from `canPass`, listed under the endpoint's `errors`. A machine token is revoked by a version the
guard compares and renewed by issuing it again. A clone in `srvkit/` is typed by the calls it makes, not as the app's
`Fetch`. And `LightX` methods live on what `fetch` returns, not on a document: a rule a service shares is a `static`
over plain values.
